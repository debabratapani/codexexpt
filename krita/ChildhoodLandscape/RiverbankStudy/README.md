# Riverbank pencil study

Open `RiverbankStudy.kra` in Krita. This is a new 1100 × 800 painting of the reference landscape with meadow in place of the house and path. The paint layers were built from scratch with textured digital pencil marks.

- Under **River**, adjust the blue and lavender underpainting, bank and tree reflections, or pale ripples independently.
- Under **Foreground tree**, adjust the trunk, darker canopy marks, and yellow-green foliage separately.
- Under **Bank trees**, adjust the willow trunks, canopies, sunlit foliage, and cool cast shadows independently.
- The meadow, distant ridge, sun, birds, and sky sit on separate layers. Reeds and wildflowers can be edited separately as well.

`preview.png` is the composite exported after reopening the native Krita file. The complete landscape in the parent directory is the earlier study.
